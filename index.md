
# Eldo Sniper — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Eldo Sniper ("the extension") is a tool for sellers on Eldorado.gg. It helps the seller send offers and chat messages for requests the seller chooses to enable.

## What the extension stores on your device
Your settings (prices, message templates, enabled categories), activity counters and license status are stored locally in your browser using `chrome.storage`. This data never leaves your device, except as described below.

## What is sent to our license server
To verify your license, the extension sends the following to our license server:
- Your **license key**.
- A **random installation ID** generated by the extension.
- A **hashed device fingerprint** built from non-identifying device traits (platform, number of CPU cores, amount of memory). The original values cannot be recovered from the hash.

The server also stores a **hashed IP address**, used only to protect the service from abuse and brute-force guessing. The raw IP address is not stored.

The extension also periodically requests the latest published version number from our server so it can tell you when an update is available. This request carries no personal data.

## What is NOT collected
- We do **not** receive your Eldorado notifications, orders, chat messages, buyer names or any other Eldorado account data. All of it is processed locally in your browser and sent only to Eldorado.gg itself, on your behalf, as you configured.
- We do not collect your Eldorado password, cookies or payment details.
- We do not sell or share data with third parties, show ads, or track your browsing.

## Permissions
- **storage / unlimitedStorage**: save your settings, the bundled game data and the activity log on your device.
- **alarms**: renew the license periodically, which is required for the extension to run.
- **Access to eldorado.gg**: read the seller's own request notifications and send offers and messages in the seller's name.

## Remote code
The extension does not download or execute any code from the internet. All code is inside the extension package.

## Data retention and deletion
License records are kept while your license is active. To request deletion of the data associated with your license key, contact us at the address below.
